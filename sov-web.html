PROJECT IN PRACTICE

Club coordination, built into the web.

01

The need

A speleological club needs a public presence and practical tools for members managing equipment, trips and archives.

02

What I built

A public website plus a role-based member workspace, with shared records backed by Supabase.

03

The practical value

Members and organisers work with the same records, while editorial, equipment and archive responsibilities have their own access.

My role: design and development of the application and its data workflows.

TWO AUDIENCES. ONE PLATFORM.

Public stories.
Member operations.

Equipment and loans

A catalogue, loan requests, equipment issue and returns. Members and equipment managers have different tools for their responsibilities.

Submissions and approvals

A member submits a record with attachments. An archivist reviews the documentation, requests changes or approves the submission.

Trips and shared records

Schedules, sign-ups and a trip archive connect preparation and activity records in one place.

Content and documentation

Article submissions, editorial publishing and galleries, plus exports of archive records and attachments for further use.

A WORKFLOW FROM THE PROJECT

Every step has its place.

A form is only the starting point. The workflow connects the person submitting data, the person reviewing it and the way those records are used afterwards.

  1. 01

    Submit

    Record, coordinates and attachments

  2. 02

    Review

    Approval or a request for changes

  3. 03

    Use the records

    Archive and documentation exports

The same approach, a different field.

This approach can be adapted to tool inventories, field reports, event registrations or internal requests. Roles, records and workflows are shaped around the organisation using them.

A DISTINCT PRODUCT IN A SHARED ECOSYSTEM

Web coordination.
Field capability.

SOV Web handles the public experience and member operations. The separately developed Android application adds maps, GPS, terrain and field documentation.
