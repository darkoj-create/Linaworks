Privacy & contact

For questions about this website and personal data, contact hello@lina-works.eu.

Inquiry preparation

The inquiry form processes your entries in your browser to compose a message. It does not send them to our server or store them in a database. You send the message yourself through your email provider. Information in a received inquiry is used to respond and discuss potential work.

Hosting & external links

The website is hosted on Vercel, which may process technical request data to deliver and secure the service. Links to LinkedIn, GitHub and other external websites are subject to those providers’ policies.

Your requests

To request access, correction or deletion of information in your inquiry, write to the address above. Please do not put confidential information in the form.

Ask Lina

The chat currently offers prepared answers and is not connected to an AI service. The conversation stays in page memory. If you choose to transfer your description to the contact form, it is temporarily stored in this browser’s sessionStorage and removed when the form loads. Starting a new conversation or reloading the page clears the displayed chat.
